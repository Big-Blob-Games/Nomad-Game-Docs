
Consistent throughout every class card is going to be different abilities and loadouts for each. 

Here are the main things affected by class cards:

Main Ability - (Right Click or Trigger)
Secondary Ability - (Q or B or whatever) 
Ultimate Ability - (R, or Spacebar or Triangle)
Passive Effect  - (No button just a passive you get during the game)
Dash - A type of dash ability (Standard roll dash for most classes)

Main Ability - Your main ability will consist of the primary weapon of your class card, for example echo's initial class card will include only his fists since he does not wake up with any weapons and fighting's using his robotics. Usually a spam able move or primary fire that will do a lot of damage. 

Secondary Ability - Usually an ability on a cooldown that will do something to boost the player archetype or add secondary damage to the character, usually something related to their class and how they operate. Potentially heals on some classes or does a utility that will work better for their class.

Ultimate - Big boom Ability that will do damage (or boost effectiveness etc.)

Passive - An effect with no button on it that will help player in game.

## Character Stats - 

1. **Severity** - Your characters current health Initially out of 5. Scales with grit by 1/2. 
2. **Signal Exposure** - This is going to be a risk/reward mechanic. Completing a level will allow you to choose from 3 different items, stat increases, or gameplay mechanic changes, each will cost you signal exposure for taking the power (or choosing not to take). Each level of signal exposure will give your character negative debuffs at the cost of power. Signal can be removed by not picking an item up on a specific level or by healing it at a Rust Mechanic/Rust Priest on a run of the game. Having high signal could benefit some character class cards.
3. **Move Speed** - How fast you can move in a match
4. **Dash Count** - How many times you can dash
5. **Ability Damage** - Damage Abilities Do
6. **Ability Crit** - Damage Abilities can Crit for
7. **Ability Crit Chance** - Crit Chance for Abilities
8. **Cooldown reduction** - How much cooldown can you reduce
9. **Shiner Multipliers** - How many shiners you can get in a run
10. **Signal Resistance** - Reduce how much signal you get from buffs at end of a round


What is the goal of the signal exposure system? This is a proposed mini game within the runs where you have to manage your signal level or risk being too overloaded with damage as you get items. Some character or artifacts may manipulate this system in order to get crazy benefits. da
