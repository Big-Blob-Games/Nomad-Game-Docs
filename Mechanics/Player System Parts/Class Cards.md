What is a class card?

A class card is what defines your Main Ability, Your Secondary Ability, your Ultimate and your passive.

Collecting class cards happen through 3 major ways. 1.) You trade with an NPC for one that you want. 2.) You collect the class cards through narrative or RP choices.  3.) Completing runs will give you shiners that will let you purchase new class cards or artifacts. 

Using a class card will let you level it up and gain alternative abilities for that class. For example 

I have a class card called ECHO, Echo will have a main ability, secondary ability, passive, and ultimate by default. As I complete runs or beat certain objectives I get stars on my class card. Once I receive X amount of stars you will then be able to unlock a second ability you can choose from the class card.

Class Card Sub Types:

1. **Echo The Prewar Crank** - Base class with a lot of customization, uses mechanical machinery to add on customizations for your character. 
2. **Wild Sharpshooter** - Guns based class, uses a lot of projectile weaponry. Ricochet ability, BEEG damage. 
3. **Waster Nomad** - A class that thrives on melee and scales off of signal exposure to deal big damage, high risk high reward style class
4. **Signal Cultist** - Uses mind bending powers and signal to manipulate enemies and make them attack each other / kill themselves. Applies status effects maybe. 
5. **Sand Weaver** - Special Class Card that manipulates the sands of Solitude and is focused on magic and defensive sand maneuvers.
6. **Shadow Shaman** - A shaman that summons minions from aberrations and dips into the irregularities void and steals powers from them. Dashes with a teleport cause its cool
7. **Shiner Hoarder** - A class that scales off of how much money you gain in a run, farming class with funny mechanics.
8. **Wyrm Warden** - Tank class specifically built to 1v1 bosses and take damage to dish it out.
9. **Rust Prophet** - Degradation and poison style class with DOTS and lots of elemental abilities. 
10. **The Loser** - Hard class with simple abilities but insanely buffed ultimate that turn you into a psycho beast that destroys everything.
