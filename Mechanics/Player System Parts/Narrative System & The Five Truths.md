
Echo has the Five Truths that will measure his ability to use 

1.) Class Cards 
2.) His relationships and choices in the game.
3.) Progression Events. 


How do we level up the Five Truths (?)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

1.) Picking certain dialogue options that relate to the certain stat. For example, I pick a Grit Dialogue Option it will then give me exp grit. 
2.) Each of the class cards could have a stat (or multiple stats) assigned to them. When you complete runs with that class card, it will level the corresponding stat. (I use a class card called Wild Shooter -> Iron class class card -> complete runs with this card, gives you iron exp at end of a run).

What are the 5 Truths?

Each truth represents a stat that can be leveled from 1-7 (All stats start at 1). As you level up stats, you gain access to Class Cards that let you change your abilities in and out (Sort of like a loadout)

![[TheFiveTruths.png]]

1. Grit - Health, Heartiness, (Warden, Tank)
2. Iron - Weapon Damage, (Gunslinger, Warrior)
3. Cipher - Signal Manipulation - (Shadow Shaman, Magic)
4. Influence - Negotiation Skills - (Diplomat, Talking to people)
5. Trail - Movement (How many dashes he can do, Explorer)

All levels increased will be from these five stats, there will be no other levels (Asides from class card level ups).
