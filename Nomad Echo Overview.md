**Genre:** Isometric RPG Rogue-lite game. 
**Name:** Nomad Echo
**Logline:** Play as a Echo, a pre-historic crank who awakens after 2000 years with forgotten memories. "The Signal" wants you and you will stop at nothing to locate him. In this choice-based isometric combat focused RPG, fight your way through mind-bending aberrations, nomadic bandits and vicious robots that are going to stop you from finding your truth on the past.
