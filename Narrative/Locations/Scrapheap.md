Scrapheap is a Junk town founded by Amias after abandoning nomadic lifestyle and embracing the slogan "Peace is where the home is" (or something like that). At the center of this scrapheap, they found a book on pre-world lore. (The lore is about an old war sometime ago). People of this town revere it as a bible and even name some of their children after the people in it. Him and his wife margraet and a few other nomadic settlers landed on this pile of scrap and stayed there for the next 200 years. After many generations, scrapheap is now a small part of the larger world of solitude with around 30 - 40 settlers, a Mayor, a merchant, and even a tavern! Many notable people from echos story
