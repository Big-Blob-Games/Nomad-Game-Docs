**Description:**
Born and raised in Scrapheap, Remington always knew how to get himself in trouble. Named after a war general in a pre-world book, he was far from the prestige that his name presented. At a young age his family died protecting the town, and he was left in care of the mayor who would adopt him. Remington was always known as the "little boy who cried wolf". As a child with a large imagination, he would go hunting for Shiners or metal and return with unbelievable stories of magical artifacts, curious curios, and troves of treasure. Because his stories were outrageous, the town did not take him seriously. Eventually, he would be cast out as a local miscreant and roam the nearby dunes for answers on his past and the secrets of Solitude. He is a self-acclaimed historian , with no real merit, but his yearning for knowledge makes him like one. On his most recent excavation into the wastes outside of scrapheap, he find our main character Echo, and befriends him on a journey which he has been waiting for all along.



**Physical Description:**

- Mid 20's, Weathered by wasteland life
- Alot of practical clothing with pockets and pouches, cool jacket
- Goggles pushed up on forehead for protection against storms
- Carries a satchel / fannypack found in waste

**Want vs Need**

Wants - Shiners, safety, to not die
Needs - Connection, a friend who wont judge him for his past, to effect something greater than his town.

I see Remington as his human perspective to Echo's situation. Asks questions that that might echo (haha) what the player would be thinking about their character. Sometimes reacts to echos choices in game and responds. Offers information about the world.

Remington in the story will grant an artifact to the player that will let them absorb shiners from a distance and not need to pick them up manually.
